## The video's method, adapted

Daniel Priestley's method has five parts. Three carry over almost unchanged, the waitlist becomes the homepage hero, and the 10x rule is not needed.

| Step in the video | HybridX version |
| --- | --- |
| Waitlist landing page | A homepage hero that starts the questionnaire. No waitlist, no sign-up wall. |
| Five diagnostic questions | Five routing questions answered before the result. Each answer also becomes demand data. |
| Export answers to an AI for audience analysis | A monthly export of anonymous answers into Claude to find who is arriving, what has stopped them and which requests have no product. |
| Short-form, then long-form, then lead form | Reels and posts, then guides and long videos, then the questionnaire as the lead form. |
| 10x rule before quitting a job | Not needed. HybridX already sells, so the answers decide the next product rather than whether to launch. |

## Where the site stands today

The homepage already offers three paths, but visitors choose alone and the site learns nothing about them.

- The hero shows three cards: the free personalised 12-week PDF plan (£0), paperback books (from £8) and the app (£5/month, 14-day free trial).
- The three plan cards below the hero (Hybrid Running Plans, Hyrox 12 Week Training Plan, Offseason Strength Plans) all link to the same free plan page.
- The books page lists four titles. Other published titles, such as 60 Hyrox Workouts, the Women's Edition, ULTRA STRENGTH and ATHX 2027, are not on it.
- Eight free tools and guides sit further down, with the store and FAQ below them.
- No question is asked, so the site cannot say which product fits a visitor or record what visitors want.

## The five questions

The first question sits in the homepage hero, so a single tap starts the questionnaire. Single-choice answers advance automatically. Question 5 is optional.

| # | Question | Options | What it decides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What are you training for? | My first Hyrox; A faster Hyrox time; ATHX 2027; XENOM; A trail ultra or marathon; General hybrid fitness | The product family. This is the video's desired outcome. |
| 2 | Where are you now? | New to structured training; Train regularly without a plan; Have raced before; Compete for podiums or age-group places. Then: home, gym or both | Beginner or advanced titles, and home or gym versions. This is the current situation. |
| 3 | What has got in the way before? (choose any) | No structure; Plans felt generic; Running is my weak spot; Plateaued; Injury or overtraining; Too little time; Too many options; Nothing yet | The reason line on the result and the secondary suggestions. This is what has failed before. |
| 4 | How do you want to follow your plan? | Free to start; On paper, one-off (books from £8); On my phone with tracking (£5/month, 14-day trial); Just tools and guides | The primary format. This replaces the video's price question with the real HybridX tiers. |
| 5 | Anything else we should know? | Free text, plus an optional race date | Pre-fills the free plan form and gives raw material for the monthly review. |

Ask for no name or email in the questions. Add a short note by the free-text box asking people to leave out health details.

## Routing rules

Question 1 picks the row, question 4 picks the column ("Free to start" and "Just tools and guides" both use the Free start column), and questions 2 and 3 adjust the choice within it. Show one primary recommendation and at most two secondary ones.

| Goal (Q1) | On paper | On my phone | Free start |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| My first Hyrox | Train for Hyrox at Home (home or both), or Hyrox 12 Week Training Plan (gym) | App, beginner Hyrox program | Free 12-week plan |
| A faster Hyrox time | Elite Hyrox Training Plan | App, advanced Hyrox program | Race Time Predictor, then the free plan |
| ATHX 2027 | ATHX 2027 book | App, if an ATHX program exists | ATHX pacing page once built |
| XENOM | Nearest live plan; log the demand | App | Free plan |
| A trail ultra or marathon | ULTRA STRENGTH | App, running program | VDOT Calculator and Build a Bigger Engine |
| General hybrid fitness | Hyrox 12 Week Training Plan | App program library | Free plan and the Hybrid Training Program page |

Rules that adjust the table:

1. Q2 "Have raced before" or "Compete" with a Hyrox goal moves the paper choice to Elite Hyrox Training Plan.
2. Q2 "Train regularly without a plan" plus Q3 "Too many options" shows one choice only and hides the secondary cards.
3. Q3 "Running is my weak spot" adds the 12-Week Running Plan for Hyrox and the VDOT Calculator as the secondary cards.
4. Q3 "No structure" or "Plateaued" adds the app as a secondary card when it was not the primary.
5. Q3 "Too little time" adds the free plan.
6. Q3 "Plans felt generic" adds the free plan, because it is personalised to race and dates.
7. A visitor who wants variety and already has a base is offered 45 Advanced Hyrox Workouts or 60 Hyrox Workouts.
8. Editions (men's or women's) are a toggle on the result, not a question in the quiz.

## Results page

The result appears at once, with no email step in front of it. The page lives at its own shareable address that encodes the answers, and is set to noindex so it stays out of search.

- **Recap line:** one sentence in the visitor's own terms, for example "First Hyrox, training at home, want a printed plan."
- **Primary card:** one product, one sentence on why it fits (taken from question 3), one button.
- **Two secondary cards:** a free tool or guide, and the same goal in a different format.
- **Optional email box:** "Email me this plan", never required. It feeds the nurture sequence.
- **Start again:** a plain link to retake the questionnaire.

Build the links so each one can be measured:

- Book buttons go through an internal redirect that logs the click and then opens the Amazon link. The Amazon short links cannot carry tracking on their own.
- The app button opens app.hybridx.club with a source tag.
- The free plan button opens the plan form with the race date and level already filled in.

Keep the wording instructional and free of hype, in the same voice as the rest of the site.

## Bringing people in

The video's short, long, lead sequence maps onto content HybridX already produces. The questionnaire takes the place of the waitlist form.

| Stage | HybridX content | Call to action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short-form | Instagram Reels (including the app ad), TikTok and Shorts clips, Reddit and blog posts | "Take the one-minute plan finder" in the bio link and pinned comment, with a source tag |
| Long-form | Guides on the site (How to Train for Hyrox, the 12-week plan structure, the rule changes guide, Build a Bigger Engine) and 6-to-60 minute videos on the same topics | A plan finder block at the end of each guide, with the goal preset to match the page |
| Lead form | The questionnaire and its result page | Free plan, book or app trial |
| Ads | Paid promotion of any short or long piece that already shows organic traction | Same questionnaire link, one source tag per ad |

Every entry point carries a source tag, so the monthly review can show which content brings in which kind of athlete.

## Build plan

The timings are suggestions for a build in Claude Code on the existing Next.js and Firebase stack. Each phase ends with a gate; do not start the next phase until it passes.

1. **Baseline and catalogue (week 1).** Record two weeks of current homepage numbers: free plan requests, app trial starts and Amazon clicks. Confirm which titles are live and map the app's programs to each goal. Gate: the product map has no Confirm rows left.
2. **Questionnaire build (weeks 1 to 2).** Hold the questions, routing rules and product catalogue in one config file, so routing changes need no code. Build the questionnaire route, result page, click redirects and anonymous answer logging in Firebase. Gate: every combination of goal and format returns a result in a test run.
3. **Homepage rebuild (weeks 2 to 3).** Build the new homepage beside the old one and split visitors between them. Gate: mobile layout checked, page speed no worse than the current page.
4. **Traffic and comparison (weeks 3 to 6).** Point short-form and long-form content at the questionnaire and read both homepage versions weekly. Gate: enough completed questionnaires to compare the two versions.
5. **First review (week 7).** Run the first Claude analysis of the answers, keep or adjust the routing, and choose the next product from the demand data.

Data handling: store each answer set anonymously, with no email unless the visitor opts in on the result page. Add a line about the questionnaire to the privacy policy, and check the wording against UK data rules before launch.

## Measuring it and using the answers

Set targets after the baseline in phase 1; the numbers below are what to track, not what to aim for.

| Metric | How it is measured | What it tells you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start rate | Homepage visitors who tap an answer to question 1 | Whether the hero question draws people in |
| Completion rate | Starts that reach a result | Whether the questions are too long or unclear |
| Result click rate | Results followed by a click on a product button | Whether the recommendation feels right |
| Actions per 100 visitors | Free plan requests, app trial starts and book click-outs, old homepage against new | Whether the rebuild beats the current page |
| Answer mix | Counts of goal, level, obstacle and format | Who is arriving and what they want |

Book sales lag, because Amazon reports them later. Use click-outs as the working measure and compare weekly totals in the KDP reports.

Once a month, export the anonymous answers and give them to Claude. Ask for the main audience groups, the top three obstacles in the visitors' own words, the goals with no matching product, and which content sources bring the best-fit visitors. The unmatched goals are the shortlist for the next title.
